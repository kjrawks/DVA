Meet the student team behind our Veterans Affairs website concept. Our goal is simple: make the first step toward finding support feel clearer and more welcoming.

Our origin story

It started with
one question.

A fictional classroom origin story

“What if finding support felt as straightforward as asking a friend?” In our fictional story, three students asked that question while brainstorming a campus project. They imagined a Veteran trying to find health care, explore education benefits, and figure out whom to contact—all while balancing everyday life.

That idea became this VA-themed website concept. The team brought together computing, communication, and design to create a welcoming starting point for learning about services. Their first planning session happened around a campus table, with one laptop, a notebook full of ideas, and a very determined coffee order.

This is the story of our fictional student team and its website project. The Department of Veterans Affairs is a real federal agency; it was not founded by this group.

A few good questions

Glad you asked.

Is this the official VA website?

No. This is a student-built educational concept. Visit VA.gov for official benefit information, eligibility requirements, and services.

Can I book a real appointment here?

No. Our site introduces the idea of clearer appointment support. Use the official appointment resources linked below to learn about scheduling actual care.

What powers the fictional team?

Teamwork, carefully organized notes, and a coffee order that somehow gets longer before every deadline.

Do I need to bring anything to the pretend office?

Bring your questions and your best ideas. A notebook is welcome; superhero capes are optional.

Can I send a message through the form?

The form is a visual mockup for class, so its send button is disabled. It does not deliver messages.
